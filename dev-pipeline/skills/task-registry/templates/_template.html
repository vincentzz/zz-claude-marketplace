2.5 Forbidden-Knowledge Boundary (forbidden to know)

Forbidden knowledge ≠ read ban: the listed information is treated as unknown even when it sits in your priors (common implementations, idiomatic algorithms), and must not take part in any decision; verdicts recognize only the "legitimate source" column. The forbidden-knowledge discipline in CLAUDE.md applies by default, and this section lists only the increment for this task; with no increment, write "baseline only".
